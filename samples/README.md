# Sample datasets

Three demo datasets for trying InsightPilot and testing it against known answers. They are generated by `generate_samples.py` with a fixed random seed, so every run produces the same files.

```bash
python samples/generate_samples.py
```

| File | Rows | What it is | Messy on purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `retail_sales.sample.csv` | 54,855 | Daily sales lines, 6 products × 4 regions, India, 2019–2025 | 3 missing days |
| `hr_employees.sample.csv` | 1,200 | One row per employee with hire and exit dates | Missing ratings, blank exit dates |
| `business_expenses.sample.csv` | 438 | Small-business expenses, 2023–2025 | `DD/MM/YYYY` dates, `₹1,23,456.00` amounts, blank vendors, 4 duplicate rows |

## Planted events (ground truth)

Later phases are tested against these. Real-world events are marked; the rest are synthetic.

### Retail sales

| When | Event | Expected effect | Used to test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 25 Mar – 31 May 2020 | COVID-19 lockdown in India (real) | Revenue falls about 65%, recovers by Aug 2020 | Anomaly detection, news context |
| 15 Apr – 31 May 2021 | COVID-19 second wave (real) | Revenue down about 35% | Anomaly detection |
| 10 days before Diwali, every year | Festive shopping (real) | Sales up about 60% | Holiday check, seasonality |
| 10 – 12 Aug 2022 | Missing days in the export | Three-day gap | Data-gap check |
| 1 Jun 2023 | Smartwatch launched | New product starts selling | Level shift, mix change |
| 1 Feb 2024 | Smartphone price cut 15% | Units up 30% | Price vs volume breakdown |
| Mar – Apr 2025 | Electronics slump in West region | West electronics revenue down about 55% | "Why did this change?" driver |

Weekends are 25% busier, there is mild yearly seasonality, and the business grows about 8% a year.

### HR employees

| Pattern | Used to test |
| --- | --- |
| Hiring push for Engineering in 2022 | Trends by hire date |
| Sales in Bengaluru: about 38% attrition vs 9% elsewhere, exits clustered in 2024 | Driver analysis, top/bottom performers |
| About 4% missing performance ratings | Quality report |

### Business expenses

| Pattern | Used to test |
| --- | --- |
| Rent rises from ₹85,000 to ₹1,10,000 in Apr 2024 | Level shift |
| One-off ₹2,45,000 equipment purchase on 18 Sep 2024 | Spike detection |
| Electricity about 35% higher in Apr–Jun | Seasonality |
| Salaries grow every month | Trend |

## Note on news

Only the COVID-19 and Diwali events are real, so they are the ones news search can find. GDELT's free search covers about the last 12 months, so 2020–2021 events need the search fallback planned for Phase 13.
